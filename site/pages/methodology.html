Ranking Methodology

Our goal is to provide a transparent and reproducible ranking of Indian computer science research institutions.

Eligible Conferences

We only consider publications in top-tier, highly-selective conferences. The list of eligible venues is sourced from the CORE Rankings, specifically those rated A* and A.

Scoring Weights

Publications are weighted based on the prestige of the conference:

  • CORE A* Conference: 4 points
  • CORE A Conference: 2 points

Author Credit Allocation

We use a fractional credit model. Credit for a single publication is divided equally among all its authors. For example:

  • A paper with 1 author receives 1.0 credit.
  • A paper with 2 authors gives each 0.5 credit.
  • A paper with 5 authors gives each 0.2 credit.

Calculating Institution Score

An institution's total score is the sum of the credits its affiliated faculty have accumulated over the selected period. The formula is:

Score = Σ (Weight × Credit)

Data Sources

The system is built on three primary data sources:

  • IRINS: Used for initial faculty and institution mapping within India.
  • DBLP: The primary source for publication data, used to verify papers and authorship.
  • CORE: Provides the conference rankings and quality signals.

Transparency

Every score is designed to be fully transparent. The goal is that every ranking can be drilled down to the individual publications and authors that contributed to it.
