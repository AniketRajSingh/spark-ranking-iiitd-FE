About SPARK

Understanding the vision behind the Scholarly Publication & Academic Ranking Knowledgebase.

The Vision

SPARK is an open-source, transparent academic ranking system designed specifically for Computer Science departments in Indian institutions. It was created to provide a localized, fair, and comprehensive assessment of CS research publications in India, overcoming the narrow filters of global systems like CSRankings.

Key Features

CORE-Rank Centric

Focuses strictly on high-impact venues (A* and A rating) from the Computing Research and Education Association (CORE) rankings.

Fractional Credit

Equal division of score amongst co-authors to ensure fair attribution and discourage artificial publication inflation.

Indian Context

Directly maps active research personnel using the IRINS (Indian Research Information Network System) infrastructure.

Open & Reproducible

Every score represents a deterministic calculation. The underlying publications can be audited directly by visitors.

Contributing & Scope

The code for the SPARK rankings knowledgebase is entirely open-source. Academic contributors are welcome to review the scoring models, submit publication verification adjustments, or integrate additional data parsing pipelines for conferences and journals.
